Adaptive Retrieval Gating for Safety-Aware Medical RAG

A replay of logged runs from my MSc dissertation (King's College London). For each of 200 MIRAGE medical multiple-choice questions you can see the closed-book answer, the always-retrieve answer, and what the training-free gate decided from the model's own signals. Nothing on this page is re-run or hand-picked: it is generated from the experiment logs.
